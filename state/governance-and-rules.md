# Governance, limits and standing directives

## Ownership and authority
- The Smith Family Trust owns 100% of Holdings. The Board is advisory.
- The Chairman (the player) decides. Deals above Rs 15,000 cr come to the Chairman; a deal of Rs 5,000 cr or more also gets a second opinion from the Chairman's Office.
- M&A office: up to Rs 2,000 cr when the Group CEO and Group COO agree. Group CEO: up to Rs 15,000 cr.
- The Vice Chairman role is vacant. When Advait was Vice Chairman he had Chairman-level authority up to Rs 30,000 cr per decision over six, then seven, companies. Since he became Chairman he holds that authority directly.

## Financial limits
- Large-acquisition ceiling: Rs 4,00,000 cr over 10 years, at most about Rs 70,000 cr a year.
- Cash floor: Rs 10,00,000 cr.
- Treasury policy (set 2279 by the Group CFO and Group CEO): target return about 5.9%, about Rs 60,00,000 cr in a group investment portfolio, no single investment above Rs 5,00,000 cr, no more than 15% in risky holdings. Any increase in dividends to the Trust comes to the Chairman.

## Standing directives
- Keep adding mines within the mining budget (base Rs 12,000 cr a year, flex up to Rs 25,000 cr when demand tests are met, no more than 25% of mining capital in one country, environment, community and conduct checks before every deal; deals above Rs 15,000 cr still come to the Chairman).
- Veer's priorities for 2278-2281 (approved): growth at least 8% (floor 7.5% if safety or conduct gates conflict), top three businesses below 50% of profit (floor 55%), Anvil, Forge and Digital Infra each top 3 globally, resilience rules (no supplier above 10% of sales), two ready successors for each Level 1 and 2A role.
- **Veer's priorities for 2282-2285 (approved Aug 2281, option B):** profit growth 7.5% (floor 7%); top three businesses below 52% of profit by 2285; fund two or three mid-sized businesses harder; Forge, Anvil and the space venture keep growing. Growth beyond the floor counts only if safety and conduct gates are met. Cash floor, yearly acquisition limit, 10% supplier cap and two ready successors per Level 1 and 2A role all stay.
- Firewall: Forge sells to outside buyers on the same terms as to group companies. The General Counsel keeps the firewall and the independent review of Blacksmith Capital's lending.
- Government Affairs: lawful influence only.

## Anvil safety rules
- The safety gate can block any launch that fails its published tests, with a 5-day appeal to the Group CEO and a 10-working-day turnaround.
- The highest-risk list includes children, weapons, large-scale deception, security, and agents that spend money, act for users or touch finances. Reviewed every six months.
- Anvil Continuous Assurance: 24/7 testing and fixing team (Hyderabad, Dublin, Austin), budget about Rs 900 cr a year (sim). Serious incidents go to the Group CEO within 24 hours.

## Sponsored answers (Anvil consumer app)
- Free tier only. Paying users never see ads. No sponsored content for under-18 users. Excluded topics: health, money, legal, politics, mental health, crisis, anything involving children.
- Ads use only the current session. No personal profile, no data sale. The notice says so, and a yearly independent audit checks it.
- Live in India, Southeast Asia, the Gulf, 14 EU countries, and 22 US states (by 2274). Auto-pause if the trust score falls more than 5 points.

## Succession rules
- Roles are open-ended. A successor needs at least a 15-year runway.
- Emergency rule: Veer is acting Chairman for up to 12 months if the Chairman cannot act, with Trust approval needed for reserved matters (deals above Rs 5,00,000 cr, selling a core business, changing the succession plan).
